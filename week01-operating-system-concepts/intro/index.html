What Is an Operating System & History

Tanenbaum Chapter 1.1 & 1.2: Resource Managers, Extended Machines, and Computing Generations.

Foundational Paradigms

An operating system serves two primary functions: acting as an extended machine (providing clean abstractions over raw hardware) and as a resource manager (multiplexing processor time, memory space, and I/O devices among competing applications).

Historical Evolution & Generations

The history of operating systems spans five distinct technological generations, transitioning from manual vacuum tube wiring to cloud-native distributed nodes.
