03. CPU Scheduling Metrics & Algorithms

Once processes are ready to execute, the CPU Scheduler determines which process gets assigned to physical hardware cores. OSTEP structures scheduling evaluation around two primary metrics: Turnaround Time (completion time minus arrival time) and Response Time (time from arrival to first scheduling execution).

Classical Scheduling Policies

  • First-In, First-Out (FIFO): Simple queue execution. Suffers from the convoy effect, where short jobs wait behind long-running tasks.
  • Shortest Job First (SJF): Executes shortest tasks first, optimizing turnaround time. However, it fails if jobs arrive asynchronously.
  • Shortest Time-to-Completion First (STCF): A preemptive variant of SJF that interrupts running tasks when a shorter job arrives.
  • Round Robin (RR): Alternates tasks in fixed time slices, prioritizing stellar interactive response time at the cost of increased turnaround time.
