Week 11: Multiprocessor Systems & Distributed Computing

Tanenbaum & Bos, Chapter 8: Multiprocessor Architectures, Cache Coherence, Gang Scheduling, Interconnects, RPC, and Distributed Middleware.

Curriculum Framework

As clock frequency scaling hit physical thermal barriers, high-performance computing shifted definitively from raw clock speed to hardware parallelism. Operating systems must coordinate concurrent execution across tight multi-core chips, non-uniform memory architectures (NUMA), loosely coupled multicomputers, and distributed networks.

Week 11 examines the mechanisms required to manage these systems: resolving hardware cache contention, coordinating atomic bus synchronization, executing thread scheduling across affinity domains, and deploying distributed abstractions that span local network nodes.
